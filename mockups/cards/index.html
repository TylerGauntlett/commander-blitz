Card face — five prototypes

Five directions for the card face, each at the four widths the client actually uses. Mana costs are the real symbols. Every direction puts the name and the cost on separate axes, which is what stops Aesi, Tyrant of Gyre Strait from fighting its own {4}{G}{U}; lands are redesigned per direction rather than tinted. Two rules apply to all five: a name wraps rather than truncating wherever there is room for a second line, and the 60px mat card drops the mana cost entirely — a permanent on an opponent's board has already been paid for, so the cost there is the one line on the card that cannot change a decision.
